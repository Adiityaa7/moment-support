Moment Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Offline evaluation version 0.6.0

Moment is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that provides reminders during app visits you choose to monitor. It is maintained through the Adiityaa7 GitHub account. Contact adityapatil206999@gmail.com for privacy questions.

This policy covers the offline 0.6.0 build. It has no reminder server, Apple push-token registration, developer analytics, advertising SDK, account, or payments. The earlier 0.5.1 policy remains available for the retired optional server prototype. Updating does not erase records that an older service operator may hold.

Information on your devices

Moment stores app labels, limits, settings, setup confirmations, and up to 200 completed app-visit records on iPhone. Visits include opening and closing times and how they ended. The watch stores manual first-step text, exercise results, local notification ordering, and pause state. These records support the app's settings, reminders, and visit summaries.

Paired-device communication

Apple WatchConnectivity carries session identifiers, ordering information, opening times, deadlines, repeat settings, first-step settings, notification permission status, and scheduling feedback between your paired devices. Reminder state uses a generic app label. These records are not sent to a Moment developer server. Local notifications are scheduled on the watch; Apple controls their presentation.

Moment does not read other apps' screens, messages, individual videos, or swipes. You select app-open and app-close automations in Shortcuts. The timer covers the whole selected app.

Permissions and controls

Notification permission is requested when you choose to enable alerts. You may decline and still use local visit recording and manual watch cues. The offline watch trial can be disabled on iPhone. A cancellation update must reach the watch before its local series stops; use Pause directly on watch if needed. No location, contacts, microphone, camera, or HealthKit permission is requested.

Retention and deletion

Clear completed iPhone visits in Privacy & support. Rules and active timers remain. Clear manual watch history separately in watch Results. Pause or disable reminders before removing either companion. Removing the apps removes their ordinary local app data; backups may retain copies under your Apple backup settings. Paired-device updates already queued may remain under the operating system's delivery lifecycle. There is no Moment server database for this offline build.

Apple, GitHub, and support

Apple's operating systems provide pairing, connectivity, local notifications, device backups, and TestFlight distribution. For TestFlight builds, Apple automatically collects beta crash logs and usage information and shares them with the app provider. This can include your name, email, device and OS details; Apple says this collection cannot be opted out of while using TestFlight. Feedback you submit is also shared. We use this information to diagnose problems and improve Moment, do not share it with third parties, and do not export it to a separate analytics service. These records remain in Apple's testing tools under Apple's retention rules. Contact the privacy address for access or deletion requests. This TestFlight collection is separate from the offline app's local visit history. Review Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

Opening support links uses the internet. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages with no added analytics scripts or cookies. GitHub may process connection information under its privacy statement. If you email support, the recipient receives your address and the information you provide. GitHub issues are public; use email for personal information and do not send passwords, signing keys, or sensitive visit history.

Advertising, audience, and changes

Moment does not sell activity data, combine it with third-party data for targeted advertising, or include advertising SDKs. This evaluation tool is intended for a general audience and is not offered as a children's service. Before adding hosted services, accounts, analytics, or payments, this policy and relevant user choices will be updated to match the implementation.
