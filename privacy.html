Moment Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Evaluation version 0.5.1

Moment is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that offers reminders during app visits you choose to monitor. It is maintained through the Adiityaa7 GitHub account. Contact the maintainer at adityapatil206999@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current evaluation app and its included single-user reminder service. A public hosted Moment service and subscriptions are not available in this version. If you connect a service run by someone else, that operator controls its server records and must explain their hosting, backups, and data practices.

Information kept on your devices

The iPhone stores your app labels, reminder limits, settings, setup confirmations, and up to 200 completed session records. A session record includes opening and closing times and how it ended. Manual first-step text and watch exercise results are stored on the watch; settings can be shared with your paired iPhone using Apple WatchConnectivity. These records are used to show your settings and visit summaries. Device backups may retain copies according to your Apple backup settings.

Information sent to a reminder service

Connecting a reminder service is optional. Before connecting, you are asked to agree to sharing the information needed for background reminders:

  • A randomly generated installation identifier, session identifiers, and update ordering information.
  • The current session's opening time, reminder deadline, expiry, and repeat settings.
  • The watch's Apple push notification token, app identifier, and notification environment.
  • Wrist-test requests and whether Apple accepted a notification request. Acceptance does not establish that you saw or felt an alert.

Version 0.5.1 uses a generic label in service messages and notification text. Your chosen app labels, screen contents, individual videos, messages, and local visit history are not sent to the reminder service. Earlier evaluation builds could include an app label; upgrading does not erase information already stored by a service operator.

The service uses this information to schedule and cancel the reminders you requested. Apple receives the notification delivery information and generic alert through Apple Push Notification service. A network connection exposes an IP address to the receiving host. The included service does not log HTTP request details, but a hosting provider or reverse proxy may keep operational logs; ask your operator about its configuration.

Permissions and choices

Moment asks for notification permission when you choose to enable alerts. You can decline and still use local visit recording and manual watch cues. You create the Shortcuts automations for the applications you select; Moment does not read other apps' screens or independently detect Shorts, Reels, or individual swipes.

Use the pause controls or turn off background wrist reminders to stop requesting further alerts. An already-sent alert may still arrive. Turning off reminders does not delete existing service records. To withdraw the service connection, open Moment on iPhone → Privacy & support → Disconnect reminder service. This removes the stored connection key, stops new service requests from that connection, and attempts to cancel the current series. In-flight requests can still finish; if cancellation cannot be confirmed, the existing series ends at its cap or expiry.

Retention and deletion

Use Privacy & support → Clear completed iPhone visits to erase completed local visit history. App rules and active timers remain. Clear manual watch history in the watch app's Results screen. Removing the app removes its ordinary local app data, while backups may retain copies. Disconnect the reminder service first to remove its saved Keychain connection key.

The evaluation server keeps only its latest state, registration, pending test, and last send result in its database, rather than a complete visit history. Its current implementation does not automatically delete all of these records when a reminder expires. They remain until replaced or removed by the service operator. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact your service operator; for a service supplied by this project's maintainer, use the privacy contact above. Backup retention must be confirmed with the operator. No specific server backup deletion period is promised by this evaluation policy.

Advertising and third parties

Moment does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs, sell activity data, or combine it with third-party data for targeted advertising. It does not request contacts, microphone, camera, location, or HealthKit access. Apple's notification and device services are used for their stated functions. The support website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses no added analytics scripts or cookies; GitHub may process connection data under its privacy statement.

Support messages and children

If you email support, the recipient receives your email address and the information you choose to provide. Use email for privacy requests; GitHub issues are public. Do not send connection keys, Apple credentials, or sensitive session data. Moment is a general-audience evaluation tool and is not offered as a children's service.

Changes

The effective date above changes when this policy changes. Before introducing a hosted service, accounts, analytics, or payments, the policy and relevant choices will be updated to describe the actual implementation.
