Moment Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Offline versions 0.6 and later

Moment provides time-based reminders for app visits you choose to connect using Shortcuts. Aditya Patil maintains Moment through the Adiityaa7 GitHub account. Contact adityapatil206999@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Moment remains in private testing. This policy covers the offline beta and the upcoming version 1.0, including optional Apple subscriptions when available. Updating this policy does not make version 1.0 available for download. The earlier 0.5.1 policy covers the retired optional server prototype; updating does not erase records an older service operator may hold.

Information on your devices

Moment stores app labels, timing limits, settings, setup choices, and up to 200 completed visits locally on iPhone or iPad. Visits include opening and closing times and how they ended. The watch stores first-step text, exercise results, notification ordering, and pause/visit-stop state. These records support reminders and visit summaries. Moment does not read other apps’ screens, messages, individual videos, or swipes. Shortcuts app-open and app-close events cover the whole selected app.

Communication between paired devices

Apple WatchConnectivity carries identifiers, ordering information, opening times, deadlines, reminder settings, first-step settings, notification permission status, and scheduling feedback between a watch and its paired iPhone. Reminder state uses a generic app label. These records are not sent to a Moment server. iPad uses local reminders and does not pair directly with Apple Watch.

Notifications and optional system alarms

Apple controls notification presentation. The optional AlarmKit mode stores alarm IDs and unfinished cancellation requests locally for cleanup. Alarm titles are generic and omit selected app names and first-step text. The bundled silent audio requests silence but cannot establish what a paired watch will play. Alarms can override silent mode and Focus, have no app-enforced duration cutoff, and use Apple’s sound and haptic behavior. App-close cancellation can be delayed; the system Stop control remains available. No alarm data is sent to a Moment server.

Permissions and choices

First launch offers an optional welcome guide. Review permissions shows an explanation before each Apple permission dialog; you can allow or decline in that dialog. A finished or skipped guide stays dismissed and can be reopened in Setup → Permissions. Previously decided permissions are changed in Settings. Watch local-notification permission is separate and is requested on watch. Allowing permission does not enable system-alarm or watch-notification mode; select those modes separately. When both are off and local notifications are allowed, ordinary device alerts can be used. No location, contacts, microphone, camera, HealthKit, or tracking permission is requested.

Apple purchases

Version 1.0 includes optional Moment Plus auto-renewable subscriptions when made available through Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment, trial eligibility, refunds, and subscription management under its own terms and privacy policy. Moment checks verified product identifiers, subscription status, and expiry/revocation information to unlock Plus. It does not receive your card details or require a Moment account. StoreKit transactions are not sent to a developer-operated server. Purchases and restoration use Apple’s internet services; local reminders do not require a hosting account.

Retention and deletion

Clear completed visits in Privacy & support; this does not delete rules or active timers. Clear manual watch history separately in watch Results. Stop alarms and pause or disable reminders before uninstalling. Removing an app removes its ordinary local app data; backups may retain copies under Apple’s backup settings. Queued paired-device updates follow the operating system’s delivery lifecycle. There is no Moment server database. Deleting Moment does not cancel an Apple subscription: use Manage Subscription or Apple Account subscription settings. Apple retains purchase records under its own policies.

Apple testing, support, and this website

Apple provides connectivity, notifications, alarms, backups, distribution, and purchases. For TestFlight builds, Apple collects beta crash logs and usage information and shares them with the app provider. This may include your name, email, device and OS details, plus feedback you send. We use beta feedback to diagnose problems, do not export it to a separate analytics service, and retain it in Apple’s testing tools under Apple’s rules. See Apple’s TestFlight privacy information.

Support links use the internet. This site uses GitHub Pages without added analytics scripts or cookies; GitHub processes connection information under its privacy statement. Email support receives your address and supplied information. We use support messages to respond and troubleshoot, retain them only as needed for that purpose or legal obligations, and accept access/deletion requests at the contact above. GitHub issues are public; use email for personal information. Never send passwords, signing keys, or sensitive visit history.

Advertising, audience, and changes

Moment has no advertising or analytics SDK, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell activity data. It is a general-purpose productivity app, not a children’s service or medical treatment. This policy and relevant choices will be updated if processing changes.
