Moment support

Choose an app limit and a next task, then get a reminder to return.

Updated 29 October 2026 · Private testing · Public release on hold

Availability

The new iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch update is being prepared for testing and has not been publicly released. Existing invited TestFlight testers can use builds made available in their group. Moment has no account or sign-in step. iPhone/iPad require iOS/iPadOS 17 or later; the companion requires watchOS 10 or later. The optional silent-audio alarm experiment requires iOS/iPadOS 26.1 or later.

First launch

Open Moment and choose Review permissions or Set up later. If you continue, each explanation leads to Apple’s permission dialog, where you can allow or decline. Reopen the guide in Setup → Permissions; change previously denied permissions in device Settings. Watch local-notification permission is requested separately on watch. Permissions alone do not enable system-alarm or watch-notification mode, install the companion, or create automations.

Choose a reminder mode

In Nudges, choose local device notifications, one standard notification on the paired watch, or the optional system-alarm experiment. Try the relevant test before relying on it. iPad uses its own local reminders; Apple Watch connects through its paired iPhone. Open both companions after installation and keep the watch nearby, unlocked, and worn for testing.

Connect Instagram and YouTube

Both start with a free 15-second preset; saved choices survive updates. Four Shortcuts automations are required. Existing automations still work; do not duplicate them.

  1. In Shortcuts → Automation, select App → Instagram → Is Opened. Choose Run Immediately and add Start Instagram timer.
  2. Create Is Closed for Instagram with Stop Instagram timer and Run Immediately.
  3. Repeat for YouTube with Start YouTube timer and Stop YouTube timer.
  4. Open and leave each app, then check the event receipts in Moment → Setup. For additional rules, use the generic Start/End app session actions with the same selected rule.

The timer covers the entire app, including messages and long videos. Moment cannot isolate Shorts or Reels, read other apps’ screens, or detect individual swipes. Automations and operating-system delivery can be delayed.

Timings and Moment Plus

Version 1.0 adds thirteen free Instagram/YouTube presets from five seconds to two hours. Plus adds exact custom durations from five seconds to 24 hours and additional app rules. Stop, pause, and privacy controls remain free. Planned US pricing is $2.99/month or $19.99/year, with a three-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Subscriptions will be available only with a supported release and Apple confirmation; this is not a current purchase offer.

When available, the purchase screen will show Apple’s actual local price, renewal period, and trial eligibility before confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. Manage or cancel in Apple Account subscriptions; Restore Purchases restores eligible access with the same Apple Account. Uninstalling does not cancel. Purchases and restore need Apple’s services; reminders remain local. See the Apple Standard EULA and privacy policy.

System-alarm experiment

The mode is off by default. Read its explanation before enabling it, allow Alarms, and try Test an alarm in 8 seconds. Return to the watch face with Moment closed on both devices. Check both devices for sound and wrist delivery. The bundled silent sound is a request, not a guarantee: Apple may use its own watch tone. Watch Silent Mode is a test condition, not proof of silence. Alarms can override Focus and silent mode.

There is no app-enforced alarm-duration maximum. The limit is the delay before the alarm, not its ringing duration. Custom watch rhythms do not apply. Leave the connected app to request cancellation, or use the system Stop button. Stop all alarms & pause reminders also pauses future visits; resume in Apps when ready. Delayed or missing close automations can leave an alarm active. Hidden or launch-locked apps cannot use AlarmKit.

Local watch notifications and foreground rhythms

The watch mode schedules one standard local notification per visit if the opening update arrives in time. A scheduling receipt does not prove presentation or a felt tap. Late updates, Focus, notification settings, lock state, pairing, and connectivity affect delivery; close updates must reach watch to cancel. Stop this visit directly on watch cancels its local reminder. No automatic repeated custom vibration is promised.

Return to my task opens Moment on watch and starts your chosen foreground rhythm. Persistent requests clicks for up to 15 seconds; Stop haptics or leaving the active watch app ends them earlier. These foreground cues are separate from system alarms.

Privacy and support

Clear completed visits in Privacy & support; clear manual watch history separately in Results. Stop alarms and pause reminders before removing the apps. No reminder server, advertising SDK, developer analytics, or Moment account is used.
