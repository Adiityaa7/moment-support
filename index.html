Moment support

A wrist nudge when an app visit passes the limit you chose.

iPhone + Apple Watch · Offline evaluation version 0.6.0 · Updated 20 October 2026

Private offline trial

Moment 0.6.0 is available to invited internal TestFlight testers. It is not a public App Store release. It uses paired-device updates and local watch notifications. No reminder host, push-service account, or subscription is needed.

Automatic watch delivery is experimental. Exact 15-second alerts and immediate stopping after exit are not guaranteed. Physical-device verification is still needed.

Set up one app

  1. Install the invited build on iPhone and its paired Apple Watch. Open Moment on watch and enable wrist alerts.
  2. Tap Test a local alert on watch, return to the watch face, and keep iPhone unlocked. Check for an alert after eight seconds. This tests a locally scheduled alert, not app detection.
  3. On iPhone, open Moment → Nudges, enable Send reminders to paired watch, and select a preset.
  4. In Shortcuts, create an App → Is Opened automation for a selected app. Use Moment's Start app session action and select the matching rule. Choose Run Immediately.
  5. Create App → Is Closed for that same app, using End app session and the same rule, also Run Immediately.
  6. Stay in that app past its limit with Moment closed on watch. Then leave before the next repeat. Record whether you felt an alert and whether reminders stopped.

If no wrist alert arrives

Check both automations, watch notification permission, Focus, haptic settings, pairing, and Bluetooth/local connectivity. Open Moment on both devices once after installation. A watch scheduling receipt confirms scheduled requests, not that a notification appeared or was felt. Opening updates that arrive over 10 seconds late or past their first deadline are skipped.

If alerts continue after you leave

A close update must reach the watch before it can cancel the series. Confirm the close automation uses the same rule. Use Pause on the watch to stop its local series for five minutes. Each series has a finite cap of at most six alerts. Phone pause also sends cancellation, but that transfer can be delayed.

Presets and detection limits

Gentle, Balanced, and Frequent choose reminder spacing and count. Background reminders use the standard watch notification vibration. Custom rhythms require the watch app active; Moment does not select the motor's frequency or strength in the background.

The timer covers the entire app. It cannot distinguish YouTube Shorts from regular videos or Instagram Reels from messages, read other apps' screens, or detect individual swipes.
