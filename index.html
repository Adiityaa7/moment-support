Before you start

The current build is an evaluation prototype, not an App Store or TestFlight release. Automatic wrist reminders need the iPhone companion, a paired Apple Watch, two Shortcuts automations per monitored app, and a configured HTTPS reminder service. A public hosted service is not included. Signed installation and Apple push setup are handled by the developer supplying your test build.

Set up one app

  1. Open Moment on the watch and enable wrist alerts if you want notifications.
  2. On iPhone, connect the reminder service supplied by your test operator in Nudges. Read the data-sharing explanation before connecting.
  3. In Shortcuts, add an App → Is Opened automation for one selected app and run Moment's Start app session action with that rule.
  4. Add an App → Is Closed automation for the same app and run End app session with the same rule. Set both automations to Run Immediately.
  5. In Moment, enable background wrist reminders and complete the wrist and exit tests. Test with Moment closed on your watch and your iPhone unlocked.

If no wrist alert arrives

Check notification permission, Focus, watch haptic settings, internet access, service status, and watch registration. Confirm that both Shortcuts actions ran. The watch app can be closed for system notifications, but custom tap patterns require it active. Notifications may be delayed or missed; exact 15-second delivery is not guaranteed.

If alerts continue after you leave

Check the close automation uses the same Moment rule as the open automation. A late or missed close update can leave a scheduled series active until its cap or expiry. An already-sent notification cannot be recalled. Use Pause all reminders or the five-minute intentional-use pause while checking setup.

What the presets change

Gentle, Balanced, and Frequent choose the gap and maximum number of reminders. Background alerts use Apple's standard notification vibration. Moment does not change the motor's frequency or strength. Monitoring covers each whole app, including regular YouTube videos and Instagram messages, not just short videos.

Privacy and data controls

Visit Privacy & support in the iPhone app to read the privacy policy, clear completed local visits, or disconnect the reminder service. Manual watch history has a separate Clear history control in Results. Service records are controlled by your test operator; contact that operator for access or deletion.
